# review-rubric.md — Blocking Gates and Diagnosis

Use this file when reviewing an existing script, storyboard, rough cut, or finished export.
Work through gates in order. Stop at the first blocking failure and give a verdict.

Do not reward production value for hiding a broken proof spine.

---

## How to run a review

1. Establish the intended viewer, promise, destination, and CTA
2. Reconstruct the proof spine from what is actually present in the asset
3. Find the first frame where the promise, action, result, or verification becomes uncertain
4. Give the earliest applicable verdict from the table below
5. Cite timestamps or exact script fragments
6. Recommend the smallest repair that restores the proof
7. Separate blocking repairs from optional polish

---

## Verdict definitions

| Verdict | Meaning | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| **Ready** | Proof spine is complete, honest, and clear | Publish — optional polish only |
| **Recut** | Footage is good but the edit hides or rushes the proof | Edit without re-recording |
| **Re-record** | Footage is wrong, missing, or cannot prove the claim | Re-record the affected section |
| **Replan** | The proof spine is broken at the concept level | Return to framing before recording |

Give the **earliest** verdict that applies. If the proof spine is broken (replan), say so —
do not suggest a recut when the concept is the problem.

---

## Gate 1 — Promise

**Question:** Does the opening tell the viewer what they are about to see?

| Check | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Hook is specific | Viewer knows what job will be demonstrated | Hook is a product description or a slogan |
| Hook begins the proof | Next on-screen action immediately delivers on the hook | Gap between hook and first relevant action |
| Viewer is identifiable | Clear who this is for | No viewer signal in the first 10 seconds |

**Failure verdict:** Recut (if footage is usable) or Replan (if the hook can't be fixed in the edit)

---

## Gate 2 — Causality

**Question:** Can the viewer reconstruct the cause-and-effect chain?

| Check | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Starting state shown | Viewer sees what exists before the action | Demo starts mid-workflow with no context |
| Action is visible | Viewer sees what triggers the result | Action is cut or too fast to register |
| Response is shown | Product visibly changes in response to the action | Cut goes directly from action to final result |
| Outcome is clear | Viewer can name what is now different | Result is ambiguous or not highlighted |

**Failure verdict:** Recut (if footage exists but is poorly edited) or Re-record (if the action or response was not captured)

---

## Gate 3 — Proof

**Question:** Can the viewer verify the claim without taking it on faith?

| Check | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Verification detail present | A field, log, file, number, or trace the viewer can read | Only a polished output with no inspectable detail |
| Verification is readable | Viewer has enough time to read the proof detail | Proof detail appears for under 2 seconds |
| No unsupported claims | Every spoken or shown claim is in the truth map | Claim present that cannot be verified from the footage |
| Conditional claims labeled | Prototype, best case, or staged data is disclosed | Conditional behavior presented as typical |

**Failure verdict:**
- Missing verification detail → Re-record
- Unreadable (too fast) → Recut
- Unsupported claim → Replan or remove the claim

---

## Gate 4 — Trust

**Question:** Does the demo accurately represent the product?

| Check | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| No misleading edits | Speed-ups and cuts are labeled | Unlabeled cut makes product look faster or smoother |
| No spliced steps | All shown steps can be performed in sequence in the real product | Two separate takes spliced as if one continuous action |
| AI output handled honestly | Input shown, output shown, control shown | AI output shown without its input or with no review step |
| Private data absent | No credentials, internal URLs, real customer data in frame | Private data visible |

**Failure verdict:** Re-record (if footage is the problem) or Recut (if an edit can remove the misleading element)

---

## Gate 5 — Readability

**Question:** Can the viewer follow and understand the demo at its real viewing size?

| Check | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Text readable at feed size | Key output text readable on phone screen | Text too small to read in the feed player |
| Muted test passes | Task, change, and result are clear with sound off | Viewer cannot follow the demo without audio |
| Narration synced | Audio matches what is on screen at every moment | Narration describes something not yet visible |
| Captions clear | Captions do not cover important UI elements | Captions placed over key output or controls |
| Result holds long enough | Viewer has time to read and understand the proof detail | Cut away before viewer can read the result |

**Failure verdict:** Recut

---

## Gate 6 — CTA

**Question:** Does the viewer know what to do next?

| Check | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| CTA is specific | One action that matches what was just shown | Multiple competing CTAs |
| CTA matches screen | The screen shown during the CTA corresponds to the action requested | "Sign up at our website" with no website visible |
| CTA is available | The thing the viewer is asked to do actually exists and works | Beta link broken, install page missing |

**Failure verdict:** Recut (change the ending) or Replan (if the product isn't ready for the CTA)

---

## Optional polish (non-blocking)

These do not block the publish verdict but improve the demo:

- Music volume balancing
- Transition smoothness
- Caption typography and animation
- Color grading or background
- Intro and outro cards
- Background music selection

Polish is applied **after** all six gates pass. Polishing before the gates pass wastes time.

---

## Cold viewer test

Show the demo to someone unfamiliar with the product without explaining anything first.

Ask only:
1. "What did the product do?"
2. "What would you do next?"

If they miss the main change: find the moment that confused them and fix that moment.
"Looks cool" is not a pass. The viewer must be able to name the job and the result.
